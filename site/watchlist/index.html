      How this list is made

      A scheduled job, with no person or AI in the loop, collects open events from public feeds and works out from the satellite's published orbit when the ground below it next comes close to each one.

      Events: NASA EONET and the agencies it lists for each event, GDACS (the United Nations and the European Commission), and the NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center. They're shown here for planning only; follow the links for the sources' own reports.

      Orbital elements: Space-Track.org. Maps: NASA Earth Observatory (Blue Marble and Black Marble). Places © OpenStreetMap contributors.
